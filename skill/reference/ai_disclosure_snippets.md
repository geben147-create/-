# AI 공시 문안 조각 (유통사 양식에 맞춰 골라 쓰기)

> 어떤 경우에도 **거짓 공시 금지**. "100% 사람이 만들었다"는 표현은 AI 생성 오디오가
> 최종 마스터에 남아 있는 한 쓸 수 없습니다.

## 1) AI 생성 오디오 + 사람 편곡 (지금 이 파이프라인의 기본형)
```
This recording originates from an AI music generator output created while the artist held a paid
subscription. The original lead vocal and lyric order were retained. A human selected among locally
generated arrangement candidates, edited individual notes, and approved the final structure.
Local automated processing performed stem estimation, transcription, rendering, mixing and mastering.
No new human vocal or instrumental performance is present in this version.
```

## 2) 위 + 사람이 부른 파트가 실제로 들어간 경우
```
... In addition, the artist recorded and performed the pre-chorus and bridge vocals heard in this
master. Raw unprocessed takes are retained. Pitch and timing correction, de-noising, EQ and
compression were applied as production tools.
```

## 3) 사람 창작 중심 + AI는 도구만
```
Lyrics, chorus melody and bridge were written by the artist. Bass and drum parts were performed by
the artist and edited for timing and velocity. AI tools were used for pitch correction, mixing and
mastering assistance only. No AI-generated audio is present in the final master.
```
(→ 이 문안을 쓰려면 **최종 마스터에서 AI 생성 오디오를 실제로 제거**해야 합니다.)
