# SHORTS_RETENTION — directing notes

The first frame is already in motion. There is no establishing shot and no
introduction; the viewer decides within a second and a half.

State the promise immediately and keep it before the halfway mark. A payoff held
back to the end is a payoff nobody sees.

Cut on movement, not on silence — matching a cut to a gesture or an impact hides
the edit and keeps momentum.

Vertical, 9:16. Compose for the centre band: the top and bottom of the frame are
covered by interface.

The final shot matches the opening frame so the loop is seamless. That single
choice does more for total watch time than any other in this pack.
