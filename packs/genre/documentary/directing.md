# DOCUMENTARY — directing notes

The narrator is never seen. Interviewees speak to an interviewer just off the
lens axis, which is what makes their account feel reported rather than
performed. A subject who turns and addresses the camera directly turns the film
into an advertisement — this single choice does more damage than any other.

Shots run longer than in any presenter-led format. The viewer is reading a place
or a document, not a face, and that takes time. Eight seconds on a held wide is
legitimate here and would be dead air anywhere else.

Every figure that appears on screen is drawn as a code layer and must trace to a
source. If there is no source, there is no number — say "most" and move on.

Music enters after the evidence, never before it.
