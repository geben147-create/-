# COMEDY_CHARACTER — directing notes

Timing is the whole craft. The reaction shot lands one beat *after* the viewer
has understood the setup — cut on the understanding, not on the line ending.

Shots are short, around 1.4 seconds. A held shot kills a joke: the viewer starts
looking at production rather than the face.

The lens is used once, on the punchline, briefly. It is a shared glance, not an
address. Repeating it spends the device.

No establishing shot. Open inside the situation; the audience will assemble the
context from the second shot.

Never explain afterwards. The button is a held beat, not a summary.
