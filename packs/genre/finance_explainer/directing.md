# FINANCE_EXPLAINER — directing notes

The chart is the lead, not a person. Where there is an anchor at all, they are a
frame around the data.

Every mark on every chart is drawn in code. This is the hardest rule in the pack
and the one most often broken: a generative model produces a chart that looks
convincing and is wrong, and a wrong chart delivered confidently is the single
worst failure this pack can produce. Axis, ticks, gridlines, labels, the series
itself — all code, composited over generated backgrounds if you want texture.

State the unit and the period every time a figure appears. A percentage without
a base is not information.

Rhythm is quick, near 2.8 seconds, but slow into the reveal (0.90) so the moment
the line lands has somewhere to sit.

Cutaways to the physical world exist to answer "what does this mean to me" —
the petrol pump, the receipt, the shelf. Without them a data video is a
spreadsheet read aloud.

Say plainly when a figure is a projection.
